Welcome to the Weather Idle Game! Click the start button below to start the game. This will begin a set of storms that occur every thirty seconds.
To survive these storms, you need to use the designated "Generate Coins" button to increase your defense for the specific storm you are about to encounter.
Defeating a wave of storms will increase your multiplier (coins/click) and idle coin generation (coins/second). Each wave will also increase the next storm's strength (intensity), so keep upgrading your defenses. Good luck!
